# Pricing notes — INTERNAL ONLY

Do not publish these numbers on vincul.ai without a deliberate pricing page decision.

## Framing
- Prefer **discovery fee** then fixed build, or monthly retainer.
- Quote in USD for remote-global buyers unless client requests otherwise.

## Starting bands (adjust per complexity)
| Offer | Discovery | Build (indicative) | Retainer |
|-------|-----------|--------------------|----------|
| CRM chatbot | modest fixed | mid project | monthly hours |
| Java upgrade | audit fixed | by module count | optional hypercare |
| Medical/logistics automation | workflow map | per workflow | ops support |

## Discount / risk levers
- Parallel access + good tests → lower risk → tighter fixed fee.
- No staging / sparse tests → time & materials or larger contingency.

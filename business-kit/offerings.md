## Packaged offers

### 1. CRM AI chatbot integration
Embed conversational AI into an existing CRM (Spring Boot + Angular/Vue preferred). Includes discovery, auth/session design, tool/API wiring to CRM entities, escalation paths, and production handover.

**In scope:** bot UX contract, backend orchestration, CRM read/write for agreed objects, logging/observability basics.  
**Out of scope (unless added):** full CRM rewrite, model fine-tuning research projects, multi-tenant SaaS product build.

### 2. Java 8 → Java 24 modernization
Staged upgrades (typically 8→11→17→21→24) with build toolchain, dependency remediation, test gates, and controlled rollouts.

**In scope:** upgrade roadmap, critical path modules, CI green on target JDK, runbook.  
**Out of scope:** greenfield rewrite, unrelated feature work disguised as “while we’re here.”

### 3. Medical & logistics automation
Booking, confirmations, invoicing, and follow-up automation for clinics and logistics ops, integrated with current tools.

**In scope:** workflow mapping, automation build, CRM/billing sync for agreed events.  
**Out of scope:** clinical decision support, regulated medical device software, warehouse WMS replacement.

### 4. AI agents & workflow automation
Custom agents that enrich, route, extract, or notify across systems the client already runs.

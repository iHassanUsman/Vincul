## Packaged offers

### 1. AI assistants inside your CRM
Embed conversational AI into the CRM and apps you already run. Includes discovery, auth/session design, tool/API wiring to CRM entities, escalation paths, and production handover.

**In scope:** assistant UX contract, orchestration, CRM read/write for agreed objects, logging/observability basics.  
**Out of scope (unless added):** full CRM rewrite, model fine-tuning research projects, multi-tenant SaaS product build.

### 2. Replace repetitive busywork with AI
Follow-ups, data entry, document handling, status chasing, reporting — any time-consuming task your team still does by hand, across sales, support, ops, finance, or admin.

**In scope:** workflow mapping, automation/agent build, sync with agreed systems of record.  
**Out of scope:** regulated clinical decision support, full ERP/WMS replacement, unbounded autonomous spend without human gates.

### 3. AI agents across your tools
Custom agents that enrich, route, extract, or notify across systems the client already runs — with human approval where it matters.

### 4. Legacy platform modernization
When old stacks block AI, staged upgrades with build toolchain, dependency remediation, test gates, and controlled rollouts (including Java LTS paths when that is the blocker).

**In scope:** upgrade roadmap, critical path modules, CI green on target runtime, runbook.  
**Out of scope:** greenfield rewrite, unrelated feature work disguised as “while we’re here.”
